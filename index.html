About

A real multilayer perceptron trained with backpropagation and mini-batch gradient descent, written in plain JavaScript - no libraries, no server. The background shows the network's decision boundary, the lines are live weights (thickness = magnitude, orange = positive, blue = negative) and each neuron's fill is its current activation.
